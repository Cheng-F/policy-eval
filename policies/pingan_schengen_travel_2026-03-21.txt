4. Regulatory Notes on Death Benefit Limits
Per China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission Document [2015] No. 90: except for aviation accidental death and accidental death caused by major natural disasters, the death benefit for an insured under age 10 shall not exceed RMB 200,000, and for an insured aged 10-18 shall not exceed RMB 500,000. The insurer is not liable for amounts exceeding this regulatory limit.
